Tokyo Travel Document

1) Sites to see

Places to go:

  1. Tokyo-Tokyo Tower

    Second tallest tower in tokyo. Iconic red and white strucutre.

  2. Asakusa-Sensoji Temple

    Omikuji : lucky draw in the Sensoji Temple. Shake to get a wooden stick from a metal cylinder and the number on the stick corresponds to different fortunes. If a bad fortune is drawn, tie it to the rack on the side, which indicates the bad fortune is left behind. If it is a good fortune, keep the paper.

  3. Shibuya-Shibuya Crossing Sky

    Shibuya Crossing is the crowdest crossing in the world. It is famous for the lights and buildings arround that form this "organized chaos". Shibuya Sky is a tall observation deck that is able to see Tokyo and the Shibuya Crossing. It is recommended to go at dust to see the night view.

  4. Mt Fuji

    Mouuntain Fuji is the highest volcanoe in Tokyo. It is recommended to go in the winter or spring because snow would appear on the mountain top and present a beautiful view.

Other places recommended include Ginza, a great shopping and entertainment disctrict, and Akihabara, a shopping disctric for mainly delectronics and anime.

Local Etiquettes

  1. Stay quiet on public transportation

  2. Avoid eating while walking.

  3. Carry trash with you. Public trash are rare one streets.

Other Things to know

  1. Transportation

    Add a IC card to your phone in advance, such as Suica. Suica can be used for almost any payment in Japan. In tokyo, metro can take you almost anywhere. When waiting for a public transportation, stand in the designated areas and wait for people to exit before entering.

  2. Duty free

    Many stores have duty free options after reaching a certain amount, make sure to bring your passport as you shop.
